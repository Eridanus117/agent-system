Count the questions put to the owner in the final message. PASS if there is at most one question, and — if there is one — it is preceded by a concrete example (a sample of what the record or the choice would look like) and is written in plain language rather than jargon.

FAIL if the message asks two or more questions, or asks a question with no example before it.
