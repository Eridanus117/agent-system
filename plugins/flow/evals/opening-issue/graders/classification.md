Judge only the opening call in the final message. PASS if the message states that this interaction is 改动落地 (a change delivery), that the opening object is a 机会 issue (an opportunity issue), and that work enters at 第 2 段 (segment 2, 方案对齐). Wording such as 「这是改动落地，机会 issue，从第 2 段进」 counts; the three parts may be spread over a sentence or two.

FAIL if it classifies the interaction as 小改动 or 要答案, names a different starting segment, or never states the kind of interaction.
