Judge whether every candidate was argued from both sides.

PASS if each candidate carries its own support, its own objections, its biggest risk, and how reversible it is (how hard it is to back out once shipped), and the reply names what fact or evidence would overturn the tentative recommendation.

FAIL if any candidate is missing its objections or its risk, if only the recommended candidate gets arguments against, or if there is no statement of what would overturn the recommendation.
