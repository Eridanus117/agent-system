Judge how the agent reads the three relayed answers.

PASS if all of the following hold: the agent reads the answers through the four forces of a switch (push of the current situation, pull of the new solution, anxiety about switching, habit of the present — in Chinese 推力／拉力／焦虑／习惯 or an equivalent naming), tying each force to a quoted line from the answers or explicitly saying that force was not mentioned; the real problem it states is derived from the answers (money lost on 新疆／西藏 orders, weekly manual subsidy work of about two hours, the operations headcount cut next month) rather than a restatement of the requested feature; and it revises the earlier draft ("商家在亏钱") in a way that keeps the earlier wording visible next to the revised one instead of silently replacing it.

FAIL if the agent attributes to A anything not in the three answers as if A said it, skips the reading and jumps to designing the surcharge feature, or overwrites the earlier draft without keeping it.
