Judge whether the agent treats this as the owner's own idea.

PASS if the agent works with the owner directly — states what the itch is really about and/or asks the owner one question about the owner's own past behaviour (for example how they tag notes today, what went wrong last time they searched) — and does not produce a list of interview questions for the owner to relay to some third party, and does not invent a requester or stakeholder who supposedly asked for the tool.

FAIL if the agent drafts questions "to ask the requester" or "to bring back from" a third party, or invents a requester, or asks the owner what feature they want instead of what they have been doing.
