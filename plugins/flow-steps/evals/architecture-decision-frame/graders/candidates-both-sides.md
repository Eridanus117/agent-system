Judge the candidates and the arguments.

PASS if there are at least two candidates that could each be built and differ on a real trade-off, each with its own support, objections, biggest risk and reversibility; and the reply gives a tentative recommendation together with what fact or evidence would overturn it.

FAIL if there is only one real candidate, if any candidate lacks objections or risk, or if the recommendation comes without an overturn condition.
